Send it by sound

An idea for where Cipher Forge goes next, beyond the seven steps. The ciphertext travels through the air as tones. Anyone nearby can hear them, but only a receiver holding the same key can turn them back into the message.

Message

F: your cipher

code: character → 0–255

hex: two base-16 digits

tones: digit → frequency

send = tones ∘ hex ∘ code ∘ F

receive = F⁻¹ ∘ code⁻¹ ∘ hex⁻¹ ∘ tones⁻¹

16 tones carry one base-16 digit each (2⁴ = 16), and two digits make one character code: 16 × 16 = 256, by the product rule.
