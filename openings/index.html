Choose an opening

Five full-screen openings for Cipher Forge. Each is a 3D scene you fly through by scrolling, with the maths on screen worked out live. Open one, scroll all the way down, then use the bar at the bottom to jump to the next.

  1. 01Rotor tunnel

    Fly down 26 rings of the alphabet. Ring k is shifted k places, and far rings spin loose, then click into place as you reach them.

    f(x) = (x + k) mod 26

  2. 02Arrow constellation

    Two rings of letters joined by beams. f(x) = 2x crashes pairs of letters together; the beams swing to 5x + 8 and every output lights once.

    not one-to-one → bijection

  3. 03The forge

    HELLO flies through three glass blocks, shift(3), multiply(5) and mirror, and the camera chases it. It comes out as BQHHS.

    F = f₃ ∘ f₂ ∘ f₁

  4. 04Relation city

    The floor is S × S. Towers rise for each pair (x, f(x)), one per row; then you dive between them and find one per column too.

    R ⊆ S × S, |R| = 26

  5. 05Keyspace warp

    Warp through the keyspace while 27 digit drums roll to the exact value of 26!, then count how long a brute-force attack would take.

    26! = 403,291,461,126,605,635,584,000,000
